Services / DaycareOS

Daycare software for enrollment and subsidy billing

DaycareOS was built for centers in Odessa and Midland. Parents enroll and book tours online, the front desk stops chasing paperwork, and the subsidy billing does the split for you.

What it does

Online enrollment forms, tour booking, a parent app for updates and payments, and billing that separates the TWC subsidy portion from what the family owes automatically.

Why it exists

Because the alternative is a stack of paper, a spreadsheet, and an evening spent working out what the state owes and what the parent owes. That is the job it removes. Most centers come to it after a subsidy audit or a parent complaint about a bill, which is a bad time to be learning a new system. Doing it in a quiet month is the easier route.

Who it is for

Licensed childcare centers in West Texas, from one location to several. If you are still taking enrollment on paper, this is the shortest path off it.

Questions

We already take enrollment on paper. How does the switch work?

The current forms become the online forms. Existing families get an invite, and new families enroll themselves from a phone.

Does it handle TWC subsidy billing properly?

That is the reason DaycareOS exists. It splits the subsidy portion from what the family owes, so nobody spends an evening reconciling it by hand.

Do parents have to download an app?

No. The parent side works in a browser, and there is an app for families who want one.

Can we run more than one location?

Yes. Each location keeps its own enrollment, staffing and billing, and the owner sees all of them in one place. Centers running two or three sites are the most common setup, and adding a location does not mean starting the paperwork again from scratch.

Managed IT for West Texas offices

Your office runs on a handful of machines, a printer nobody can fix, and email that has to work. Managed IT means somebody is watching all of it, and answering the phone when it breaks.

What it covers

Monitoring and alerts on the machines and network, patching so the known holes get closed, helpdesk for the day to day, backup checks, and security basics like MFA and endpoint protection.

Who it is for

Businesses with five to fifty staff and no IT person. Oilfield offices, clinics, law firms, accounting practices, real estate offices. If the office manager is currently the IT department, this is the thing that stops that.

How it starts

A walk through of what you have now, in person or remote. We tell you what is broken, what is risky, and what it costs to fix. No contract before that conversation.

Managed IT for West Texas offices

Your office runs on a handful of machines, a printer nobody can fix, and email that has to work. Managed IT means somebody is watching all of it, and answering the phone when it breaks.

What it covers

Monitoring and alerts on the machines and network, patching so the known holes get closed, helpdesk for the day to day, backup checks, and security basics like MFA and endpoint protection.

Who it is for

Businesses with five to fifty staff and no IT person. Oilfield offices, clinics, law firms, accounting practices, real estate offices. If the office manager is currently the IT department, this is the thing that stops that.

How it starts

A walk through of what you have now, in person or remote. We tell you what is broken, what is risky, and what it costs to fix. No contract before that conversation.

Questions

Do we have to sign a year-long contract?

No. It starts with a walkthrough of what you already run, and you get the findings whether or not you hire us.

We already have someone who fixes things occasionally.

That is common and it usually works until it doesn't. Managed IT covers the monitoring, patching and backups that a part-time arrangement quietly skips.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many machines and what shape the network is in. We scope it first and quote after that, not the other way round.

Do you cover Odessa and Midland only?

We cover the Permian Basin: Midland, Odessa, Andrews, Big Spring, Monahans and out toward Pecos. Onsite visits are scheduled by area, so outside Midland and Odessa a few days' notice is normal.

Fifteen minutes, no pitch. Tell me what you are running now and I will tell you whether this is the right fix or not.
